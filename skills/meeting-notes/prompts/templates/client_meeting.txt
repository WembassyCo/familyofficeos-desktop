CLIENT MEETING TEMPLATE

Focus on:
- Client's current concerns and priorities
- Proposals or updates presented
- Client feedback (positive and negative)
- Objections or hesitations
- Timeline expectations
- Relationship health indicators

Extract:
- Client sentiment (positive/neutral/negative)
- Upsell or cross-sell opportunities
- At-risk indicators (churn signals)
- Next steps with client-facing owner
- Follow-up materials to send
